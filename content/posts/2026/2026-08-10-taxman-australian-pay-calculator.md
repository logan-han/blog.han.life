There are plenty of Australian pay calculators around, but they all bury the answer under ads, pop-ups, or a broker form that wants your email first. I got sick of it and built my own, same as [my tax invoice generator](https://invoice.han.life).

It covers take-home pay, contract rate versus salary, and mortgage repayments with stamp duty. No backend, so nothing you type leaves your browser, and inputs live in the URL if you want to bookmark them.

🔗 Check it out here: https://tax.han.life

💻 Source code on GitHub: https://github.com/logan-han/taxman
